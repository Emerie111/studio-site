How an ingredient becomes an ad

A label is written for regulators. An ad is written for people. I translate one into the other in three steps: the term as it appears on the label, the idea behind it in plain words, and the opening line that makes someone stop and listen. The explanation is what helps a shopper understand why the product matters, which is what lets the ad do its job. Everything stays education only, with no promised results.
